Reading this page

  • The flop and turn show the solver's stored average strategy, one row per individual hand (no buckets).
  • The river is not stored. The first time you reach a river for a given turn and betting line, it is solved on this machine for both players and all river cards at once; after that it is instant.
  • Betting is no-limit with an ante of 1 and 100 behind: a bet of half the pot or the pot, and one raise per street. Each board is worth half the pot.
  • The flops are fixed. Pick the turn and river cards when the betting reaches them.
  • Bar height in a cell is the share of that hand class still in the shown player's range; the colors split it by action. Combos sharing a card with a board are left out, and a fully blocked cell is hatched.
  • Range weights multiply the player's own action probabilities along the path. Opponent card removal is not applied.
