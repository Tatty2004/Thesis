Reading this page

  • Every number is the solver's average strategy at this checkpoint. The solve is young and still moving, so treat it as a snapshot, not an equilibrium.
  • Betting is limit: a bet and one raise per street, with bets of 2 on the flop and 4 on the turn and river. Each board is worth half the pot. Labels and amounts come from the solver's own betting template.
  • The flops are fixed (board A Ah7c2d, board B KsKd9h). Pick the turn and river cards when the betting reaches them.
  • Bar height in a cell is the share of that hand class still in the shown player's range; the colors split it by action. Blocked combos (sharing a card with a board) are left out, and a fully blocked cell is hatched.
  • On the river the strategy is stored per equity bucket (200 per turn deal, fitted on (equity A, equity B)), so hands in one bucket play the same mix. The inspector shows each combo's bucket and equities, computed here exactly as the solver computes them.
  • Range weights multiply the player's own action probabilities along the path. Opponent card removal is not applied.
  • Probabilities are stored to 1/255 (about 0.4%), so a shown frequency can differ from the solver's by up to that much.
  • No EVs: checkpoints store strategies only, so there are no hand values to show.
